# Jimeng Product Video Prompts

## Build the brief

1. Identify the product, the feature to showcase, and the intended mood. Inspect attached images for packaging geometry, materials, product count, colors, logo placement, and legible text. Preserve brand names as supplied, including Chinese names inside an English prompt. When reference images disagree, follow the user's chosen source for product identity.
2. Default to an English prompt for a 15-second, 16:9 photorealistic ad unless the user specifies otherwise. If a detail is missing, choose a reversible staging assumption. If the product is missing and no image or context identifies it, use a clear product placeholder and keep the rest usable.
3. When adapting someone else's prompt, extract the desired effect, then design a new sequence around the user's product. Change the opening image, shot progression, signature action, setting, and transitions where appropriate. Write fresh language.

## Design the shots

- Give each time block one main action, one camera move, and one visible reason for the shot. Cover the full duration with contiguous ranges. For 15 seconds, start with four or five shots and reserve the last 1–2 seconds for a readable hero frame.
- Build the visual hook from a real feature. Use raking light for embossed paper or engraving, a controlled reflection for polished metal, or a lateral move to reveal layered packaging. Apply shallow depth of field to macro details and keep the full product readable in the hero shot.
- Pick one signature motion that serves the brief, such as a suspended turn, a low-angle rise, or an overhead reveal. When using an orbit, set its direction, arc, and final angle. Prefer a short arc when a single reference only shows the front. Use a full rotation only when requested or when the necessary angles are established.
- Place cuts and sound accents on specific actions. Use a short whoosh on a camera move or an impact on a landing if it fits. Keep motion blur on the moving shot and restore clarity at the reveal. Describe audio as an editing cue when audio generation is outside the brief.
- Keep the original proportions, print, brand colors, product count, and material behavior consistent across shots. Anchor each unfamiliar surface in the supplied views. Leave any unseen sides out of frame where accurate branding matters. Keep added captions, props, and graphic overlays under the user's direction.

## Deliver a paste-ready prompt

Return one English fenced text block with a brief product and visual direction, the timecoded shots, and concise product-consistency constraints. If an external setting matters, put exact requested duration, aspect ratio, or resolution in a short note outside the prompt without claiming platform support. For a prompt-only request, deliver just the prompt. For revisions, make the requested changes and carry forward the product facts and timing that still apply.

Check that the timing adds up, camera and product actions are distinguishable, every shot contributes something visible, and the final frame displays the correct product. Check that the prompt has an independent sequence and wording when a reference prompt was supplied.
